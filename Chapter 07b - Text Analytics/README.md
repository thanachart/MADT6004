Tokenize Thai + English reviews, surface complaint themes

- **Local Jupyter** (after cloning the repo): `jupyter notebook` in `notebooks/` and open the `.ipynb`. Make sure you've cloned the full repo so that `Integrated Data Analytics Exercise/data/brewlab.db` exists alongside this folder.

## What you'll do
Reviews are a free-text leading indicator. Numbers say everything's fine; reviews tell you when it isn't.

You will:
1. Load the reviews
2. Tokenize Thai + English with PyThaiNLP
3. Get top tokens by sentiment
4. Identify the branch whose negative review share is rising
